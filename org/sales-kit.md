# Sales kit (Sunday Agency)
Short words on purpose. Use {name} {company} {link}. The sample is free. Say so every time.

## The offer (say it the same way every time)
- We build you a website. The sample is free. You do not pay to look.
- Starter $97 a month. Growth $197 a month. Pro $297 a month. $0 setup.
- Month to month. Cancel any month. No contract.
- We keep your domain name in our account. If you leave, we move it to you.
- If a payment fails, the site turns off 14 days later. Pay and it comes back.
- Leads are emailed to you. We never sell your data.
- Google reviews on the site come from Google. We never make up reviews.
- Terms: /terms. Privacy: /privacy. (Plain-words drafts; lawyer review first.)

## Rules (read before every call or text)
1. Business numbers only. Never a home or personal cell you found by guessing.
2. Check Do-Not-Call before you call or text. If they are on it, do not.
3. "Stop", "no thanks", "don't contact me": stop at once. Write it in the notes. Never contact again.
4. No fake deadlines. No "only 2 spots left". No fake or made-up reviews.
5. Always say: "It is a free sample. You owe nothing."
6. Call 8am to 8pm their time only.

## 10-minute demo (use their phone, not your laptop)
1. (1 min) "I made you a free sample site. Can I text you the link?" Send {link}. Ask them to open it on their phone.
   (Make the link in the maker with "Share with the owner".)
2. (2 min) Thumb through it together. Their name, their city, their phone number. Tap Call: their own phone rings.
3. (2 min) Slide the before/after. Tap "Ask about this": the form picks the job for them.
4. (1 min) Point to the star rating. "Real Google reviews go here. We never make them up."
5. (1 min) Ask: "What would you change? Pictures? Colors? Words?" Write it down.
6. (2 min) The plan: "Starter is $97 a month, $0 setup, month to month. Cancel any month. Growth $197 and Pro $297 add more for when you want it."
7. (1 min) Close: "Want it live this week? I need 10 job photos and 10 minutes." If they say no, say thanks and stop.

## 5 objections
- **"I get work from word of mouth."** Good. Those people Google you before they call. This makes sure they find you.
- **"I already have a site."** Open both on your phone. Which one would you call?
- **"$97 is a lot."** One extra job a year pays for it. No contract. Cancel any month.
- **"I am too busy."** That is why I do it. I need 10 photos and 10 minutes.
- **"Facebook is enough."** Keep it. Google shows sites for "{trade} near me", not Facebook posts.
If they say "stop" at any point: "Understood. Sorry to bother you." Then stop.

## Templates (each says it is a free sample)
**Text (first touch)**
Hi {name}, I made {company} a free sample website. You owe nothing. Take a look: {link}
Reply STOP and I will not text again.

**Facebook / Instagram DM**
Hi {name}, I like the work {company} posts. I made a free sample website for you to look at: {link}
No cost, no strings. If it is not for you, just say so and I will stop.

**Email**
Subject: A free sample website for {company}
Hi {name},
I built {company} a free sample website so you can see it before you decide anything: {link}
It has a big call button, before and after sliders, and a form that emails you the lead.
If you like it: Starter is $97 a month, $0 setup, month to month. Cancel any month.
If not, reply "no" and I will not write again.

**Voicemail (30 seconds)**
"Hi {name}, this is {me}. I made {company} a free sample website. You owe nothing. I am texting you the link. If you want to talk, call me back at {phone}. If you do not want calls, just tell me and I will stop. Thanks."

## Follow-up schedule
- **Day 0:** text or DM with the link. Same day, if you called: voicemail.
- **Day 2:** short text. "{name}, did you get a look at the {company} sample? Happy to change pictures or colors."
- **Day 5:** email with the link and the plan. Call one time.
- **Day 10:** last note. "Last message from me. The free sample stays up a few more days: {link}. Reply any time." Then stop for good.
Any "stop" or "no" ends the schedule right away. Say the real days the link lasts, not a made-up deadline.

## Upsell ladder (offer one step only after the lead emails show the site works)
1. Starter $97 to Growth $197 to Pro $297 (more pages, more changes, more help).
2. Google Business Profile setup and photo posts.
3. Review requests: text customers a Google review link after each job.
4. Local SEO: city and service pages, a monthly report.
5. Google Local Services Ads or search ads, managed for you. Ad spend is paid by them.
6. Seasonal page (storm season, spring fence, summer wash).
Never push a step they did not ask about twice. Real numbers only: show their calls and forms, not guesses.
