## Offer model (from his own terms and pricing pages)
- Main plan $297/mo: site (10-20 pages), CRM, business phone number, missed-call text back, review request funnel, one-click campaigns, lead follow-up, on-site SEO.
- Second tier $750/mo: same plus Google Local Service Ads management. Google Ads and deeper SEO are quote-only upsells.
- No setup fee stated. Month-to-month, or prepaid 3 months (includes a Google Business Profile tune-up he values at $400). No refunds, billed in advance, texts and calls billed from a wallet.
- Site is licensed, not owned: it stops when the plan stops. (We should decide our own stance and state it plainly.)
- Close process: 20-minute demo call, onboarding form, build in 7-10 days, 25-minute launch call. He says he sends a short video before calls to cut no-shows (secondary source).
- Course pitch is the same offer resold under your own brand on GoHighLevel, found through cold outreach, then Meta ads.

## Contractor sample sites on /our-work (image mockups, no live URLs)
Checked three: Dr Pressure (power washing), Eternity (pools), ABC Pressure Washing.
- Pattern A (2 of 3): hero photo background, headline left, lead form card on the right inside the hero (name, phone, email, short message, consent checkbox, submit). Form is above the fold.
- Pattern B (pool): full-bleed hero photo, centered-left H1 "Let Us Design & Build Your Dream Pool!", one amber "Get Free Quote" button, no form at the top.
- Header: logo left, 4-5 text links, phone number as a solid pill button on the right (red, cyan, or amber).
- Trust: Google and Facebook star ratings under the hero button; a "Leave a Review" floating tab; "veteran-owned" in the subhead.
- Below hero: services as a grid of photo cards, then icon/badge row, then a map and service areas (seen on mobile mockups). Each site is one strong brand color on a dark base.
- Mockups show monitor, laptop, tablet and phone side by side: he sells "works on every screen".

## Apply to our sites (max 10)
1. Put the phone number in the header as a solid, colored pill that is a tap-to-call link on mobile.
2. Offer both hero variants: form-in-hero (name, phone, short message) for urgent trades; single "Get Free Quote" button for design-led trades (pools, remodels).
3. Keep the lead form to 4 fields plus a plain-language consent line.
4. Show Google star rating and review count right under the hero button, using real numbers only.
5. Services as photo-card grid straight after the hero, then badges/licenses, then service-area map.
6. Add a missed-call text-back and review-request flow as named features in the monthly plan.
7. Price card pattern: one main plan with a clear list, one higher tier for ads management, ads as quote-only.
8. Offer a 3-month prepaid option with a concrete bonus (a profile tune-up) in place of a setup fee.
9. Show the process as 3 steps with days (call, build in a set number of days, launch call).
10. Send a 2-minute video with the demo link before the call, and state site ownership/cancel terms in one sentence on the offer page.
