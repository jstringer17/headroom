# Inspiration: Northpeak Roofing concept by JZ Studios (saved 2026-10-21)
URL: https://jz-studios.vercel.app/work/northpeak/index.html (public demo, fictional company). Ideas only. Do NOT copy its words, logo, images or code.

What makes it feel premium (our words):
- Warm cream page (about #ece5dc) with near-black type and ONE burnt-orange accent used only for tiny eyebrow labels and the logo.
- Huge high-contrast display serif (Didone style), all caps, tight leading, 3-4 lines for the hero headline.
- Tiny eyebrow above the headline: a short rule line plus spaced caps ("ROOFING CONTRACTOR - TOWN").
- Hero photo is cut into a house/roof silhouette (clip-path), not a plain rectangle.
- Second hero beat: full-bleed dusk photo with a giant serif line over it and one small paragraph aligned bottom-right.
- Trust strip: 4 cells in one rounded outlined box, each with a tiny roof-line icon, bold title, one grey sentence (licensed and insured, free inspections, written warranty, insurance claims). Only claim what the owner ticks.
- Work section: each project is a rounded photo card with the client surname as a giant serif word across it, then a 4-column meta row (WHERE / JOB / ROOF / ON SITE) under thin rules, plus a "01 / 03" counter pill. Honest label: "Sample projects: homes and names are fictional."
- Services as a dark section: large serif rows with numbers (01-05), the active row opens with a short paragraph, and a rounded photo on the right changes with the row.
- Process: "How it goes", four short steps, photo of a crew member on the left, serif headline.
- Sticky translucent nav: serif logo + "Demo site" pill, 3 links, phone, one dark pill button.
- Everything: rounded corners (about 24px) on photos and cards, generous space, no gradients, no icons beyond tiny line icons.

For our maker: make an "Editorial" look as an extra look/theme alongside the cinematic one. Photos still come from the CEO's Higgsfield pictures (placeholder art until then, clearly labeled). No fake work photos, no invented reviews or claims, "Sample site" label stays.
