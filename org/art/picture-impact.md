# Which real pictures lift the score (CEO list, 2026-10-22)

The qa-reviewer says the drawn placeholders cost about 0.3 to 0.4 of the average. These three rubric lines carry most of that:
- **Line 1:** the first screen stops you.
- **Line 2:** photos and motion feel premium.
- **Line 9:** the default path looks finished and close to the reference.

The lifts below are estimates on the 1–5 rubric, judged from the reviewer's notes; they are not measured. Slot names match org/art/higgsfield-kit.md.

## What it adds up to
- Only the first picture in each list: about +0.2 on the average. Roofing and pressure washing would sit near the 4.3 bar.
- The first three items in each list (5–6 pictures per trade): about +0.35 on the average. All three trades are expected to pass.
- The optional service pictures (`svc-*`) only tidy the service rows. Make them last, if ever.
