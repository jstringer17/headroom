2. Never buy credits, never change the plan, never upgrade, never enter payment details. If Higgsfield asks for any of that, stop and tell the CEO.
3. Stop and ask the CEO for a "go" after picture 4 (the story set) and again after picture 9. Do not start another trade. Max 2 retries per picture.
4. Do not sign in to anything else. Do not use stock photos or anyone else's pictures. Do not use the people in a picture: no people, no faces, no hands, no text, no logos.

## Settings
- Model: **Nano Banana Pro** (pick **GPT Image 2** if Nano Banana Pro is not available). Resolution 2k. Aspect ratio **16:9**. One image per run.
- "Make it new" pictures: type the prompt, no reference image.
- "Use Edit" pictures: select the previous picture as the reference image, then paste the prompt. Keep editing the newest one. This keeps the same yard, camera and light.
- Optional for consistency: for `vinyl` and `rail`, use the `cedar` picture as the reference image so the yard matches.
- After each picture: look at it. Reject it and retry if it has any people, text, logos, warped fence lines, or a plastic or cartoon look. The fence lines must be straight and the grass, light and shadows believable.
