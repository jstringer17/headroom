## D. Update: $35 price test relaxed

Only one product changed status; the rest fail on repeat purchase, not price.

- **Re-admitted:** pet fountain + proprietary filter (56). Generic filters sell for $1 to $6, so only a private-label cartridge makes a moat.
- **Now viable as refill models (under $35):** Supply, Philips OneBlade, Boveda, Blueland, Filtered Beauty and InFlow (links in section C).
- **Still rejected:** air fryer liners, dog toe grips, pee pads, lift harness, poop bags, dryer balls, posture corrector, car bundle (no refill moat); GLP-1 products (regulated); nail grinder (battery).
